### 1. Snap Inc. (Featured Account)

#### Project Title
```text
90-Second Account Brief: Snap Inc. (Enterprise & AR Ad Strategy)
```

#### Project Description (498 / 500 max characters)
```text
Built a 90-second account brief on Snap Inc. for an Account Manager interview using Clay's AI agent (Claygent) and the 'Direct and Verify' methodology. The most interesting finding was how fast Snapchat+ scaled to 12M+ subscribers, providing high-margin balance to volatile video ad revenue. I verified every metric against SEC 10-K filings and Snap Newsroom before locking the brief. Having a verified, 182-word brief gives me instant recall on revenue engines and high-impact interview questions.
```

#### "Which Claim Needed the Most Digging to Verify?"
> **The Claim:** Snap's fifth-generation Spectacles smart glasses and its commercial distribution model.  
> **The Digging & Resolution:** When Claygent initially ran, it hallucinated that Snap had released new consumer smart glasses for retail purchase, conflating previous consumer Spectacles (which historically resulted in a ~$40M inventory write-down) with recent news. To verify this, I opened the primary source: Snap's official Partner Summit keynote (September 17, 2024) and their official press release on `newsroom.snap.com`. The digging revealed a vital distinction: **these glasses are not available for consumer retail purchase**. Instead, Snap launched them strictly as a **developer platform for Snap OS**, requiring a **$99/month subscription with a 1-year commitment** to cultivate spatial AR lens creators. Catching this in the "Verify" half was critical: pitching consumer retail glasses would demonstrate poor company knowledge, while recognizing the developer ecosystem strategy demonstrates commercial maturity.
